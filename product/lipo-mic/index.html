Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Lipotropic MIC+B12 Injections.

MIC stands for Methionine, Inositol, and Choline — three lipotropic compounds that help break down fat and support liver function. Combined with Vitamin B12, this injection creates a powerful formula that accelerates fat metabolism, boosts energy, and improves mood.

Lipotropic compounds directly target fat cells, stimulating the release of fat from adipose tissue and supporting the liver's ability to process and eliminate fat. When combined with a healthy diet, MIC+B12 injections can meaningfully accelerate fat loss.

MIC+B12 is often used as a complement to GLP-1 medications like Semaglutide or Tirzepatide. Your provider will evaluate whether combining treatments is appropriate for your specific health profile during your telehealth consultation.

MIC+B12 injections are generally well-tolerated. Some patients experience mild injection site sensitivity. Rarely, patients may experience digestive discomfort. Your provider will review your medical history to ensure this treatment is appropriate for you.
