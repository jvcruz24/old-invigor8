Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Compounded Tirzepatide.

Compounded Tirzepatide is a dual GLP-1 and GIP receptor agonist — the same active ingredient found in Mounjaro® and Zepbound®. Its dual-action mechanism produces superior weight loss results compared to single GLP-1 medications.

Clinical trials show Tirzepatide produces up to 20.9% average body weight loss, compared to 16.8% for Semaglutide. Tirzepatide targets two hormone receptors (GLP-1 and GIP), while Semaglutide targets one (GLP-1 only), making Tirzepatide more potent for most patients.

All patients begin at the lowest dose (2.5mg) and titrate upward based on tolerance and response. Your provider will guide your dosing schedule.

Tirzepatide is ideal for patients who want the most powerful weight loss results and are committed to a structured program. Your licensed provider will evaluate your health history during your telehealth consultation to determine if it's appropriate for you.
