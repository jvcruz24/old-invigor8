Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NAD+ Injections.

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme essential for energy metabolism and cellular repair. NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, contributing to fatigue, cognitive decline, and accelerated aging. Restoring NAD+ levels through injections replenishes this vital molecule at a cellular level.

Oral NAD+ precursors like NMN and NR have poor bioavailability and are degraded in the digestive system before reaching cells. NAD+ injections bypass this entirely, delivering the molecule directly into the bloodstream for maximum effectiveness.

NAD+ therapy is beneficial for patients experiencing chronic fatigue, brain fog, age-related decline, athletic recovery challenges, mood disorders, or those simply wanting to optimize their health and longevity.

Many patients report improved mental clarity and energy within the first 1–2 weeks of treatment. Anti-aging and recovery benefits typically become more pronounced with continued use over 4–8 weeks.
