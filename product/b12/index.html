Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Methylcobalamin B12 Injections.

Methylcobalamin is the active, most bioavailable form of Vitamin B12. Unlike cyanocobalamin (the synthetic form found in most supplements), methylcobalamin is immediately usable by the body without conversion, making injections significantly more effective than oral supplements.

Oral B12 supplements are poorly absorbed — especially for people with digestive issues or B12 deficiency. Injections bypass the digestive system and deliver B12 directly into the bloodstream, ensuring full absorption and faster results.

Patients experiencing fatigue, brain fog, mood changes, or those on a weight loss program benefit most. B12 deficiency is common in people over 50, vegans, vegetarians, and those taking certain medications like metformin.

Many patients report improved energy and mood within 24–48 hours of their first injection. Full benefits, including improved focus and metabolic support, typically develop over 2–4 weeks of consistent use.
