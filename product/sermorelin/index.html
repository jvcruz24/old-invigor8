Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sermorelin Injections.

Sermorelin is a synthetic analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). Rather than introducing synthetic HGH directly, Sermorelin stimulates your pituitary gland to naturally produce and release your own growth hormone — making it a safer, more physiologically appropriate alternative.

Unlike synthetic Human Growth Hormone (HGH), Sermorelin works with your body's natural feedback mechanisms. Your pituitary gland releases only as much growth hormone as your body naturally would, reducing the risk of side effects associated with synthetic HGH. It's also significantly more affordable.

Sermorelin is well-suited for adults experiencing age-related declines in energy, muscle mass, sleep quality, and recovery. Athletes looking to optimize performance and recovery also benefit significantly. Your provider will determine eligibility during your telehealth consultation.

Sermorelin works gradually with your body's natural cycles. Most patients notice improved sleep quality within 2–3 weeks. Noticeable improvements in body composition, energy, and recovery typically develop over 3–6 months of consistent use.
